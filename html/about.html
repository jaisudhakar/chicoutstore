Our story

Made for little people.

ChicOut is a shop for babies, toddlers and little kids — and for the grown-ups who dress them. We look for clothes that feel soft from the very first wear and stay comfortable through naps, play dates and messy dinners.

That means breathable cotton, easy snaps and stretchy waists, prints that make kids smile, and festive outfits that are as comfy as they are cute. It also means first shoes with soft, flexible soles for tiny feet.

From everyday sets to birthday dresses and newborn gift boxes, every piece is chosen to make getting dressed a little easier — and a lot more fun.
